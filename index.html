How to play

placeholder tutorial. Need to make better later

Press rock to get a rock. Use rocks to buy upgrades. Sometimes you can also find a stone. Stones have more upgrades. When you click the stone 100 times, you will get a short 2x to stones and one energy stone used to upgrade the boost.

Resets:

Bricks

Resets rocks, rock upgrades, stones and stone upgrades for bricks.
Bricks earned is based on current rocks and stones

Build

reset everything bricks does, as well as bricks and brick upgrades. Unlocks new resets and doubles rocks and energy stones permanently. Can only be done once

Copper

All 3 metals earned is based on rocks and stones. Better metals require more.

Resets everything build does for copper. Copper can be used for general upgrades.

Silver

resets everything build does for silver. Silver can be used for large buffs.

Gold

resets everything build does for gold. Gold can be used for buying automation upgrades.
